Does Sieve need AI?

No. Local inventory, secret matching, and advisory checks work without AI. A compatible assistant is optional for interpreting saved evidence and helping you review fixes.

Does monitoring run while my Mac sleeps?

No. Scheduled checks need Sieve running and your Mac awake, with project access and monitoring consent still enabled. Check the last successful check and coverage status before acting.

Can it prove my app is secure?

No. Sieve reports evidence within supported local formats and available advisory coverage. Missing findings are not proof of safety, a disappearing advisory is not a verified fix, and local dependencies may differ from deployed code.
