Privacy policy

Updated October 28, 2026

Your app records

Workbill stores the records you create on your device. You do not need to create a Workbill account. Records and exported copies may also be included in backups or destinations you manage. Deleting the app does not remove copies you previously exported or retained in backups.

Purchases

Apple handles App Store payments and purchase transactions. The app uses purchase status to provide paid features. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Exports and feedback

When you export, copy or share records, the destination you choose handles those copies. There is no support mailbox, so we do not receive messages from you. An App Store review is public and is handled by Apple.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information when serving these pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement. The screenshot gallery does not send your interactions to an analytics service.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

We do not run a support mailbox. Questions or concerns about this policy can be raised in an App Store review, which we read. Reviews are public, so please leave out anything private.
